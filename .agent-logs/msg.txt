feat(backend): reserve a relayed intent before the payment challenge

Implements ADR-0067.

The idempotency check ran before the 402 challenge but only read, while the
intent that would claim the key was written after settlement. Two concurrent
requests carrying the same key both read nothing, both were challenged, and
both settled a payment; the second was then correctly refused, after its money
had moved.

Creating the intent is now what claims the key, and it happens before any
challenge. The database arbitrates the race by the same insert-then-interpret-
the-conflict move recordRelayedIntent already used, so the loser is refused
before it is asked to pay.

An intent gains payment_required, so the absence of a payment reference stops
carrying two opposite meanings: a free relayed write owes nothing, while a
payment-required intent with no reference is a reservation awaiting settlement.
Both settlement sweeps and the registry's terminal-state decision now read that
column rather than inferring from absence.

A reservation is structurally non-broadcastable: status 'reserved' is excluded
from every query that hands an intent to the chain, its placeholder operation
cannot resolve to a completion handler, and runRelayedIntent refuses one
outright.

The middleware records the EIP-3009 authorization it is about to settle before
it posts to the facilitator, never after. A process that dies mid-settlement
then leaves a reservation naming the exact authorization, so the expiry sweep
asks the token contract about that specific nonce instead of inferring from
absence. Such a record is readable only as an attempt; nothing refunds,
credits or broadcasts on it.

Reservations expire on a TTL and are swept, and are never expired without first
establishing that no payment landed against them.
