# Moderation

Owner: Community Lead. Backup: on-call Senior Moderator. Escalate credible threats, child-safety
issues, legal requests, or suspected staff abuse to the Security Lead. Review enforcement trends,
exceptions, and overdue actions monthly.

Use the least restrictive action that stops harm and preserves evidence: remove content, warn,
slow mode, timeout, kick, then ban. Active phishing, malware, credible threats, and coordinated
evasion may move directly to removal and ban.

For every nontrivial action, record the actor, rule, evidence link, action, duration, and appeal
path in the staff moderation record. Do not debate enforcement in public channels. A moderator
who is personally involved hands the decision to another moderator.

Escalate credible physical threats, child-safety issues, or legal requests immediately to the
Security Lead and preserve evidence without redistributing it through Discord.
