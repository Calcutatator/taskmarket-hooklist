# Moderation appeals

Owner: Community Lead. Backup: a Senior Moderator who did not make the original decision. Escalate
credible threats, child-safety issues, legal requests, or suspected staff abuse to the Security
Lead. Review appeal timeliness and overturned-decision patterns monthly.

The `report-a-problem` channel links to the private appeal intake. The person who made the original
decision must not be the sole appeal reviewer. A Senior Moderator reviews the rule, evidence,
proportionality, duration, prior relevant actions, and new information.

Acknowledge an appeal within two staffed business days and provide the outcome within seven when
practical. Outcomes are uphold, reduce, reverse, or request more information. Record the decision
and rationale in the restricted moderation record. Public pressure, wallet balance, contributor
status, or product spending do not change the standard.
