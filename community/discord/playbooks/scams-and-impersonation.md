# Scams and impersonation

Owner: Security Lead. Backup: on-call Senior Moderator. Escalate active credential compromise,
member-wide exposure, or malicious integration behavior to the incident commander. Review every
confirmed case within two business days and test the phishing response quarterly.

1. Remove malicious links and quarantine the account.
2. Capture message, account, channel, timestamp, and destination evidence in the restricted record.
3. Ban confirmed phishing or impersonation accounts and invalidate affected invite links.
4. Post a concise warning in `status` or `announcements` only when members face ongoing exposure.
5. Rotate compromised webhook or app credentials and review the Discord audit log.
6. Direct affected members to the private support form and relevant wallet-provider guidance.

Never repeat an active phishing URL as a clickable link. Never claim that Taskmarket can reverse
an on-chain transaction.
