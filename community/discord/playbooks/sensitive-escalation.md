# Sensitive support escalation

Owner: Community Lead. Backup: Security Lead. Escalate active compromise, privacy exposure, or an
unavailable private intake to the incident commander. Sample sanitized escalations monthly and
exercise the private route quarterly.

Stop public troubleshooting when a case involves account access, payments, identity, abuse,
personal data, private tasks, protected submissions, disputes, or security evidence.

1. Remove exposed sensitive material where moderation policy permits and preserve only the required
   restricted evidence.
2. Give the member the approved private link from `report-a-problem`; do not move the evidence into
   staff chat.
3. Record only the private case reference and routing state in the public thread.
4. State that Taskmarket and blockchain records remain authoritative and that Discord staff do not
   adjudicate disputes.
5. Sanitize and close the public thread, or keep a generic resolution when it helps future users.

Never request a seed phrase, private key, access token, password, identity document, or wallet
signature in Discord.
