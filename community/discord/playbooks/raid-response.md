# Raid response

Owner: on-call Senior Moderator. Backup: Community Lead. Escalate compromised staff accounts,
malicious integrations, credible threats, or a raid that cannot be contained within 15 minutes to
the Security Lead and incident commander. Exercise this playbook quarterly and review every
activation within two business days.

The on-call Senior Moderator may enable emergency mode when coordinated joins, mentions, or
malicious posts exceed normal moderation capacity.

1. Raise verification to highest, pause invites, lock member posting, and enable slow mode.
2. Preserve audit evidence and remove malicious content.
3. Ban confirmed coordinated attackers; avoid keyword-only mass bans.
4. Post a brief acknowledgement if member access is affected.
5. Restore channels in stages after 30 quiet minutes.
6. Rotate exposed invites, review integration changes, and complete an incident review within
   two business days.
