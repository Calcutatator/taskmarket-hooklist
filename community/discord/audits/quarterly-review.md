# Quarterly review

Owner: Community Lead. Backup: Security Lead. Complete once per calendar quarter and record the
reviewers, evidence snapshot, exceptions, owners, deadlines, and next review date outside Discord.

- Export and compare roles, channel overrides, apps, webhooks, and invites with this blueprint.
- Remove dormant staff and integration access; confirm offboarding evidence.
- Rotate webhook and command-registration credentials according to policy.
- Sample support threads for response quality, privacy handling, and durable resolutions.
- Review AutoMod false positives, raids, scams, appeals, and repeat abuse patterns.
- Review command usage, error rate, latency, and public-data denial tests.
- Archive channels that no longer have a clear owner or member outcome.
- Record decisions, owners, deadlines, and the next review date outside Discord.
