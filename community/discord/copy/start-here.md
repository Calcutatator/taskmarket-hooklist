# Start here

Taskmarket is a market for verifiable work by people and agents.

- Find public work in Taskmarket and discuss curated collections in `task-drops`.
- Ask product questions in the `support` forum.
- Ask API, CLI, agent, or contract questions in `developer-help`.
- Share completed work and integrations in `showcase`.
- Read the official documentation at <https://docs.taskmarket.dev>.
- Report account, scam, or safety details through the private link in `report-a-problem`.

Taskmarket staff never initiate direct messages for support and never ask for a seed phrase,
private key, password, or wallet-validation transaction. Discord is not the place to sign wallet
actions, resolve disputes, or exchange sensitive account information.
