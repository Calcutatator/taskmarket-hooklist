# Rules and safety

1. Be constructive. Harassment, hate, threats, and targeted abuse are not allowed.
2. Do not spam, manipulate engagement, or repeatedly promote unrelated products.
3. Do not impersonate Taskmarket, staff, partners, or other members.
4. Do not post malware, phishing links, credential requests, or deceptive wallet prompts.
5. Never request or share seed phrases, private keys, passwords, access tokens, or personal data.
6. Keep support in the support forums. Move sensitive details to the canonical private support form.
7. Do not broker payments or disputes in Discord. Use Taskmarket's product flows and records.
8. Respect intellectual property and disclose material conflicts or affiliations.

Moderators may remove content, limit access, timeout, kick, or ban accounts to protect the
community. Reports are reviewed by people. Appeals use the private support form linked in
`report-a-problem`.
