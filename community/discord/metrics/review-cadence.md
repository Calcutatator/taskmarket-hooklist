# Metrics review cadence

Community and Support review service levels, unresolved threads, duplicate questions, and safety
events weekly. Product, Developer Experience, Community, and Security review the aggregate measures
in `definitions.md` monthly. Owners review access, apps, webhooks, retention, and measurement need
quarterly.

Every review samples real threads for quality rather than treating counts as truth. Record decisions
and owners outside Discord. Remove a metric when no current decision depends on it; never add
message-content surveillance to improve reporting.
